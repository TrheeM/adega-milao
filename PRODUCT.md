## Platform

web

## Stack

Static single-page site: one `index.html` (HTML/CSS/JS, no build step) plus a local `assets/` folder. Libraries via CDN `<script>`: GSAP (+ScrollTrigger) and Three.js (ES module via import map). Pinned by the user's pasted "Soda" recreation prompt (single self-contained file, CDN libs).

## Users

Customers in Lorena/SP who want drinks now: whisky, gin, tequila/licor, vodka, beer, energy drinks, flavored ice for "copão", combos. They browse on the phone (Instagram traffic), pick a drink, and want to know if it is in stock before going or ordering delivery.

## Product Purpose

Online catalog for Adega Milão (Depósito de Bebidas Milão). Every product leads to WhatsApp (+55 12 98152-0564) with a ready message asking if that specific drink is available. Success = taps on "tem disponível?" that turn into WhatsApp conversations.
